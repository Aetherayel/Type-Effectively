# Community feedback and contributions

**Type Effectively is an owner-directed project, not an open-contribution project.**

The source is visible so visitors can inspect how the model works. Public visibility does not grant write access, a role in governance, or permission to reuse the source beyond [LICENSE](LICENSE) and applicable platform terms.

## Feedback is welcome

Use the [community feedback form](https://github.com/Aetherayel/Type-Effectively/issues/new?template=build-feedback.md) for reproducible bugs, inconsistent outputs, unclear explanations, accessibility problems, and well-supported observations about the model.

A useful model report identifies the version, relevant output, what seems inconsistent, and why. A screenshot or exported card is optional. Do not publish raw personality scores or private share links unless you deliberately intend to disclose them.

A surprising result is not automatically a scoring bug. An individual preference or a familiar type stereotype is not sufficient evidence for changing a mapping.

## Development is invitation-only

- Please do not submit unsolicited pull requests, code patches, balance changes, or personality-to-type mapping rewrites.
- The maintainer may close unsolicited pull requests without review or discussion.
- Feedback does not establish a commitment to implement, respond to, or debate a suggestion.
- Code contributions require explicit advance invitation and agreement on scope and contribution terms.
- The maintainer retains final authority over the model, implementation, product direction, and releases.

## Modeling standards

Proposals are evaluated for coherence, differentiation, interpretability, balance and delight. Any invited change to scoring must identify the underlying evidence, preserve deterministic behavior, explain the tradeoffs, and include tests for distribution, edge cases, and mechanical consistency.

Respect the distinction between personality theory and authored game mechanics. This is an entertainment project, not a diagnosis, clinical assessment, or authoritative account of anyone's identity.

Security concerns belong in the private process described in [SECURITY.md](SECURITY.md), not public feedback.
