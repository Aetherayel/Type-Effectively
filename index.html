How the numbers work

Every build receives exactly 535 base stat points. The spread emphasizes relative tendencies, not overall worth: higher Attack is not inherently better than lower Attack.

The six base stats blend 70% Big Five, 20% Enneagram, and 10% 16Personalities scores. The final stats are bounded and normalized, so they are not direct psychological test scores.

Nature, OCEAN & modifiers

Your four-letter personality type determines your Nature, which raises one non-HP stat by 10% and lowers another by 10%. When modified, the radar shows the adjusted number above its base value.

OCEAN stands for Openness, Conscientiousness, Extraversion, Agreeableness, and Neuroticism (emotional sensitivity). On shared cards, H means a score of 50 or higher and L means below 50; “Estimated” means no Big Five scores were entered.

These are authored, game-inspired interpretations—not validated measures of resilience, intelligence, character, or mental health.
