# Type Effectively

Know yourself. Know your weaknesses. Blame type matchups.

Ever wondered what kind of Pokémon your personality would make you?

No? Well, we made the calculator anyway.

Type Effectively combines MBTI, Enneagram tritypes, and Big Five personality traits to generate your very own Pokémon build: dual typing, Nature, 535 base stat points, abilities, and a moveset tailored to your psychological tendencies.

Are you a defensive wall? A glass cannon? An emotionally unavailable Ice/Steel type?

There's only one way to find out.

Disclaimer: This is not a scientifically validated personality assessment. But neither is determining your emotional resilience by checking your Special Defense stat.
